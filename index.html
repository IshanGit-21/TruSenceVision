How It Works
STEP 01

Register

Capture student photo. Face encoded via dlib into encodings.pkl for fast matching.

STEP 02

Start Session

Begin class. Continuous checks fire every 15 seconds throughout the entire class.

STEP 03

Detect & Match

OpenCV DNN detects faces every 15 frames. face_recognition matches known students.

STEP 04

Mark Attendance

≥75% checks = Present. All data logged to SQLite with full timestamps.
